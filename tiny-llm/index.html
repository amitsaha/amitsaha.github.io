Tiny LLM Lab

This page was generated with assistance from an LLM. The other pages on this site are handwritten.

Ask a specific question about something a post states. The model runs entirely in your browser, and the text you paste stays on this machine.

The first question downloads FLAN-T5 base (about 280 MB). After that, this browser reuses it. Long posts are shortened to about a thousand characters so they fit in the model’s context window.
